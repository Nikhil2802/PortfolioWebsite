## Direction contract

THESIS: One collision, two jets. The page is a single reconstructed detector event whose origin is one engineer and whose two jets, secure infrastructure and shipped software, carry identical weight and identical scale. It refuses the arrangement every developer portfolio ships, a dark hero over a grid of same-size project cards, and it refuses the ranked CV that quietly makes one half a footnote.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black vacuum (#07090D) rendered as a live, cheap fragment shader. Concentric detector arcs in muted steel blue (#5B7C99). Two signal track colours, infrastructure yellow (#F2D02C) and software cyan (#39C0CF), with calorimeter red (#D93B30) for energy bars. Archivo carries structure and the display voice; Martian Mono carries the measurement layer. Everything is instrument chrome: hairline arcs, engraved letterspaced labels pinned to tracks, tabular figures, latching controls. No cards, no rounded product-UI containers, no eyebrows, no glow-as-decoration.

STORY: The visitor lands on one event already reconstructed and reads a name, one positioning line, and two jets at equal weight. They select a track to isolate it, read real evidence at increasing depth as the layers move outward, meet the degree as a calorimeter of fourteen real grades, and arrive at a console that sends a message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed vacuum. Top-left, NIKHIL PATEL in Archivo at commanding display scale, capped at 6rem, with one static positioning line beneath naming both halves and Fujitsu. Centre-left sits the interaction point; two jets fan right through three labelled concentric arcs, upper jet yellow and infrastructure, lower jet cyan and software, each track carrying a real tool or product name. A left instrument rail holds run metadata in mono. Lower-left, a live readout reports whatever the cursor is over. Primary controls, contact and projects, sit as latching instrument buttons under the positioning line.

FORM: Event Display, the collider event display, user-pinned over the roll's assignment; challenger id `scientific-notation-particle-detector-event-display`, seed key `0c370129`. Shader Portal is the losing competitive challenger and donates exactly two things: the vacuum ground as a real fragment shader, and the cursor as an instrument that reads the field under it. Signature interaction: the latch. Hover or focus isolates a track to full signal colour, drops every other track, and stamps its identity into the readout; click or Enter latches it until dismissed. Motion grammar: one authored reconstruction on load, tracks drawing outward from the origin with exponential ease-out, content visible by default, fully still under prefers-reduced-motion. Measurements are never invented: the calorimeter bars are the fourteen real module grades, and no physics units are fabricated anywhere.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
